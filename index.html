Executive Summary

The account transitioned to our management on September 1, 2026, with a limited, unstructured setup — 167 legacy campaigns, of which only 21 were actively spending; the remaining 146 were paused remnants with no clear structure or product-level control. Rather than disturb the campaigns already running at a low, efficient ACOS, we left "Old Campaigns" untouched and built a new, scalable structure from the ground up — organized by product, with dedicated portfolios and a controlled Auto + Exact testing approach before expanding into broader match types. The new structure went live within days of takeover and has already generated efficient volume.

What We Changed

This is where most of the strategic work happened over the period. Rather than simply adjusting bids on the existing setup, we rebuilt the account's foundation.

PRODUCT-LEVEL PORTFOLIOS

Each priority ASIN (or ASIN group) now has its own dedicated portfolio — 9 created so far, covering duvet sets, fitted sheets, sauna kilts, all-season duvets, pillow cases and briefs.

OLD VS. NEW SEPARATION

Legacy "Old Campaigns" intentionally isolated and left running as-is — low ACOS, no need to touch.

AUTO "CATCH-ALL"

Auto targeting consolidated across all product portfolios into a single campaign, used to surface converting search terms and product targets before committing budget to Exact.

CONTROLLED TESTING

Each new product portfolio launched conservatively with Auto + Exact only, low starting bids — validating demand before opening up spend.

DATA-DRIVEN EXACT EXPANSION

Converting search terms identified via Auto are being promoted into dedicated Exact campaigns as they prove themselves.

NEXT PLANNED

Phrase/Broad expansion, Sponsored Brands, low-bid category catch-all campaigns, and cross-sell campaigns linking complementary products (see Section 9).

Structure logic: Product → Portfolio → Campaign type (Auto / Exact, expanding to Phrase / Broad / SB) → Targeting → Bid optimization.

Recommended Actions

"sommerbettdecke" (summer duvet) terms are the single biggest leak, all inside the legacy "Bettwaren – März 25" campaign — non-seasonal for the account's core bedding range and worth negating there directly. Add the remaining non-converting terms above as negative exact in their respective legacy campaigns ("Neu Elli 97/3 Spannbettlaken", "amzkey – Neu Doubleface Bettwäsche"). Monitor the high-ACOS-but-converting terms for one more cycle before deciding on negation vs. bid reduction. The new structure is intentionally excluded here — its Auto campaigns are still in early discovery, where clicks without orders are expected data-gathering, not waste.

Scoped to the untouched legacy portfolio only, combined wasted spend is 3.2% over the full 60-day window — confirming that even the account's oldest, unrestructured campaigns run reasonably tight, and reinforcing that the $200/day constraint is genuinely a scaling problem, not a targeting-quality one.

Upcoming Seasonal Opportunity

The account is entering a more relevant seasonal period, particularly for bedding and duvet-related products. The current data should be viewed as a foundation-building phase — the new structure has only been live two weeks, and early signals (ACOS, ROAS, search term conversion) are already strong heading into peak demand.

Before Peak

  • Expand keyword coverage on proven products, especially sauna kilts and bedding
  • Build dedicated Exact campaigns for validated search terms
  • Continue collecting conversion data via Auto before committing further Exact budget
  • Build negative keyword lists from the wasted-spend analysis
  • Request a budget cap increase — $200/day is hit consistently

During Peak

  • Increase budgets on proven campaigns (bedding, sauna kilts)
  • Protect high-converting terms from becoming budget-limited
  • Monitor CPC inflation and adjust bids on ACOS / CVR
  • Reallocate budget toward highest-potential products as data confirms winners

After Peak

  • Reduce spend on underperforming targets
  • Maintain proven evergreen campaigns
  • Analyze seasonal search-term data to prepare next cycle

Conclusion

Efficient early results

6.52% ACOS and 15.3x ROAS on the new campaigns since takeover, with wasted spend in the untouched legacy portfolio at just 3.2%.

Better-positioned account

Clearer segmentation by product, validated demand data from Auto targeting, and a conservative testing approach that kept efficiency high.

Primary constraint: budget, not performance

The $200/day cap is limiting how far the new structure can grow — this is now the priority to unblock.

Next phase: controlled expansion

Scale proven products and search terms while expanding into Phrase, Broad, Sponsored Brands, and cross-sell — maintaining the same efficiency discipline.
